Visualize a curve of affordability for budgeting with a loan. This uses the "28 / 36 rule" to define affordability. In that, housing costs shall not exceed 28% of gross income and all combined debt payments (including housing) shall not exceed 36%.

As of 2024, the NEA reports that the average monthly salary for new teachers in the US is about $3,600 (~$43K / year). In the example below, a person pays various other forms of debt amounting to $500 every month. This stands for the credit card debt, student, auto, and other personal loans.

Hover over & click the matrices below to display the total loan cost based on interest rates (matrix left to right), as well as the down payment (matrix up & down). Adjust the settings to the left to increase granularity or view a different interest rate or down payment range.
